- **Empty `heap.replace(x)` now raises a catchable `IndexError`** instead of
  aborting the process (I#1579, decisions/lang/1579-heap-replace-catchable.md),
  matching `heap.pop()` / `heap.peek()` on an empty heap and resolving the first
  policy item held under #1566. A `try`/`except IndexError` handles it, `finally`
  and context cleanup run, and execution continues; the uncaught form still
  prints the standard diagnostic ("heap replace on an empty heap") and exits 1.
  `heap.pushpop(x)`, which is defined on the empty heap, is unchanged.
