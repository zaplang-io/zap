- **Variadic `tuple[T]` — a dynamic-length immutable sequence (Phase 1)** (PR#1473, decisions/lang/1473-vartuple-phase1.md, RFC discussion #1472). The one-type-argument sibling of the fixed heterogeneous tuple: `(A, B)` stays fixed, while `tuple[T]` / `tuple(iterable)` is a dynamic-length, immutable, homogeneous sequence (Python's `tuple(iterable)` result). This phase adds the type, construction (`tuple(iterable)`, `tuple[T]()`, `tuple[T](iterable)`), indexing (`t[i]`, negative allowed), iteration, `len`, and the Python-parens repr (`(1, 2, 3)` / `(1,)` / `()`). Equality + hashing (dict/set key), ordering, concatenation and slicing are later phases of epic #1461.
