- **Allocation-failure policy reconciled onto catchable `MemoryError`** (#1581,
  decisions/lang/1581-memoryerror-reconciliation.md; builds on #882, #1566).
  A **bounded or temporary** allocation failure now raises a catchable
  `MemoryError` that a local `try`/`except MemoryError` (or `except Exception`)
  handles, with `finally` / `with` cleanup on the unwind path. The three
  temp-buffer wrappers the #1566 sweep left on plain-call lowerings —
  `str.expandtabs`, `str.translate`, and `http.url` — now lower through an invoke
  so the raise reaches the handler instead of terminating the process, and their
  runtime OOM raises are typed `MemoryError` (`str.expandtabs`/`translate` share
  `str.format_map`'s buffer, which already carried the invoke edge). The GC's own
  infrastructure allocation — the collector maps, the `ZAP_GC_HEAP_LIMIT` caps,
  and a general heap exhaustion observed at an unrooted allocation site — remains
  a clean fail-stop `abort()` (`zap: out of memory`), unchanged from D-0119;
  SPEC §10 now states the split. The uncaught form still reports the normal
  diagnostic and exits 1.
