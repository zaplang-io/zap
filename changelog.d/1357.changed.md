- **`time.sleep` / `time.sleep_ns` / `time.sleep_for` are now scheduler-aware
  and cancellable on a coroutine** (I#1357,
  decisions/runtime/1357-cancellable-coroutine-sleep.md). Called on a coroutine
  with an owning `scope:`, a sleep now parks the coroutine on the netpoller timer
  until its monotonic deadline — freeing the worker so sibling coroutines run
  during the wait instead of being blocked by a pinned OS thread — and is a
  cancellation point: a `scope(timeout=)` deadline, a `cancel_on` signal, or a
  sibling failure wakes the sleep at once and raises `Cancelled` (surfaced as
  `ScopeTimeout` under a timed scope). On a native thread (`main`), a coroutine
  with no owning scope, or a non-Linux build, the sleeps keep the previous
  blocking, nanosecond-exact behavior; the cancellable path is millisecond-class.
  A zero / negative / already-past duration is a cooperative checkpoint rather
  than a silent no-op. The `time.sleep*` surface is unchanged. Generalizing
  M-freeing to every coroutine sleep is tracked in I#1360.
