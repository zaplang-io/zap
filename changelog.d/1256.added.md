- **Comptime diagnostics — `comptime.error(at, msg)` + derive/instantiation
  backtrace (E2)** (I#1256, epic #1249,
  decisions/lang/1256-comptime-diagnostics.md). The compile-time engine gains
  the HQ4 diagnostics layer. A library generator can abort compilation with
  `comptime.error(msg)` / `comptime.error(at, msg)` (the Zig `@compileError`
  model), pointing at the user's offending declaration via a reflection record's
  new `.span` accessor (`field.span` / `variant.span` / `attr.span`); the abort
  carries the author's own message, never the "not available at comptime"
  wording. A new const-eval error category claims `ZAP7xxx`: `ZAP7001` (comptime
  evaluation error), `ZAP7002` (error raised by comptime code), and `ZAP7003`
  (the dedicated, consumer-grade "cannot derive X for T"). A `DeriveBacktrace`
  renders the `required because of …` instantiation chain (Rust's model),
  threading the per-field/per-element spans from the `@derive` consumer down
  through the unroller (#1251) and kind-switch (#1253) to the failing point; a
  `@derive` that cannot apply points at the user's own field (never a
  comptime-internal symbol), and a fault inside a generator renders a dual-span
  error citing both the generator line and the instantiation site — never a
  panic. Compiler-internal and host-tested; no parser surface, and the wiring
  into a real `@derive` generator lands with E3. No behavior change for existing
  programs.
