- **Package-defined exception families via `@exception`** (I#1278,
  decisions/lang/1278-exception-families.md; discussion #1280). A `struct` marked
  `@exception` is a catchable exception (raiseable and nameable in `except`);
  `@exception(Parent)` groups it under another `@exception` struct so
  `except Parent` catches the whole family. Families may be multi-level, each
  member has one parent (an acyclic forest), and a family root is itself an
  ordinary raiseable exception. The marker adds only a catch-family edge — no
  field or method inheritance (general inheritance stays the deferred #66).
  Cross-module family catch works (`except pkg.Base` catches a member raised
  inside `pkg`). **Breaking (pre-1.0):** only an `@exception` struct may now be
  raised or caught — raising or catching a plain struct is a compile error
  (ZAP5139 / ZAP5140), closing the `raise Point("oops")` footgun. New
  diagnostics: ZAP5136–ZAP5140.
