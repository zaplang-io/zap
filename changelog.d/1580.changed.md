- **CSPRNG entropy failure is a catchable `OSError`** (#1580,
  decisions/lang/1580-csprng-oserror.md). A failure to obtain entropy from the OS
  CSPRNG (`getentropy`/`/dev/urandom` on POSIX, `BCryptGenRandom` on Windows)
  previously `abort()`-ed the process — even inside a matching `try`/`except`. It
  now raises a catchable `OSError` (equivalently `IOError`) across every
  CSPRNG-backed surface: `crypto.random_bytes`/`token_hex`/`token_urlsafe`/
  `randbelow`/`choice`, and `uuid.uuid1`/`uuid4`/`uuid7`/`nanoid`. The raising path
  discards partial output — a caller never observes a partially-filled buffer —
  and the uncaught form still reports the normal diagnostic and exits 1. Resolves
  the second policy item held under #1566.
