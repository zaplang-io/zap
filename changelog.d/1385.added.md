- **`bytearray` — a mutable, growable byte buffer** (I#1385,
  decisions/lang/1385-bytearray.md). The mutable sibling of the immutable `bytes`,
  for building up and patching bytes in place. Constructors: `bytearray()`,
  `bytearray(n)` (zero-filled), `bytearray(b)` from `bytes`, `bytearray(other)`
  copying another bytearray, `bytearray([…])` from a `list[int]`, and
  `bytearray(s)` encoding a `str` as UTF-8. Supports indexed get/set
  (`a[i]` / `a[i] = v`, an `int64` byte `0..=255`), `append(int)`,
  `extend(bytes | bytearray | list[int])`, `+=`, and `a + b` (a new bytearray);
  plus `bytes(a)` (an immutable copy), `len(a)`, byte-wise iteration (yielding
  ints), content `==` / `!=` against `bytes` and `bytearray`, and a slice read
  `a[i:j]`. An out-of-range index raises a catchable `IndexError`; a byte outside
  `0..=255` raises a catchable `ValueError`. `bytearray(b)` copies, so mutating the
  result never writes the source `bytes`. Slice-assignment-with-resize, `del a[i]`,
  and `memoryview` are deferred to their own follow-ups.
