- **`tuple[T]` ordering, concatenation, and slicing** (PR#1475, decisions/lang/1475-vartuple-ordering-concat-slice.md). A variadic tuple now supports lexicographic `< <= > >=` (shorter-is-less on a common prefix), `tuple[T] + tuple[T]` concatenation into a fresh tuple, and `t[a:b(:c)]` slicing (negative indices/step included) yielding a `tuple[T]` — all matching CPython's tuple. Completes the `tuple[T]` sequence surface in the variadic-tuple epic #1461 (construct/index/iterate/len/repr/==/hash/order/concat/slice); exhaustive goldens + SPEC are #1476.
