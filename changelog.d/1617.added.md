- **Comptime `for` unroll-breadth cap + cost telemetry** (I#1617, epic #1553, decisions/lang/1617-comptime-iteration-cap.md). A `comptime for` that would unroll more than `DEFAULT_ITER_CAP` (100 000) elements now fails fast with a clean `ZAP7001` budget diagnostic naming the breadth and the cap, instead of exploding the specialized output — a fixed hard limit (like the step and depth caps, never a tunable quota) that layers under them (each loop's breadth vs. total work vs. nesting). The comptime engine also exposes compile-time cost telemetry — `steps_used()` and `unrolled_iterations()` on the evaluator, alongside the existing `type_info(T)` `MemoStats` — a deterministic, comparable metric. Comptime/reflection surface, Experimental; no effect on real derives, which are orders of magnitude under the cap.
