- **Core `DateTime` carries subsecond precision** (I#1148,
  decisions/stdlib/1148-subsecond-datetime.md). `DateTime` gains an
  `int64 nanosecond` field (nanoseconds-of-second, `0..=999_999_999`), readable
  as `dt.nanosecond`. `datetime.replace` gains two optional keyword fields —
  `microsecond=N` (Python-faithful, scaled ×1000) and `nanosecond=N` (full
  precision); at most one per call. `datetime.isoformat` / `datetime.rfc3339`
  render the fraction (6 digits for a whole number of microseconds, exactly
  CPython `isoformat`; 9 digits for finer nanosecond precision; omitted when
  zero), and `datetime.parse` / `datetime.rfc3339_parse` read a variable-length
  1–9-digit fraction. Whole-second values are byte-for-byte unchanged. Unblocks
  dateutil-compatible microsecond deltas in zaplang-io/datetime-utils#2 (#324).
