- **A returning decorator's synthesized handler now reaches its parameter past
  optional named metadata** (I#1626, decisions/lang/1626-adapter-metadata-binding.md).
  The handler parameter typed as the argparse seam `fn([C,] Namespace) -> R` may
  appear anywhere in the receiver method's signature, and the adapter is passed to
  it by name, so a Click-style factory works: `command(str name="",
  fn(Namespace) handler)` with `@cli.command` desugars to
  `cli.command(handler=greet$adapter)` (and `@cli.command(name="alias")` to
  `cli.command(name="alias", handler=greet$adapter)`) instead of filling `name`
  with the handler (`ZAP6001`) or not recognizing the seam when the handler is not
  last (`ZAP4021`). A raw handler (no seam, #1527) and the outer object-threading
  decorators keep the trailing positional argument, unchanged.
