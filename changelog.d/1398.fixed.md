- **A nested `scope(timeout=)` deadline now wakes a `time.sleep` (or any blocked
  wait) in its own body** (I#1398,
  decisions/runtime/1398-nested-scope-cancels-body.md). A cancellation point —
  `time.sleep*`, a channel send/receive, a socket read/write, an explicit
  `checkpoint()` — running inside a lexically nested `scope:` is now bound to that
  innermost scope rather than to the coroutine's spawn scope, so the nested
  scope's own deadline cancels the wait at once. Previously the inner deadline
  could not select the wait and the sleep ran to its full duration while an
  enclosing scope reported the coroutine still parked. The D-0094 labelling is
  preserved in both directions: a scope's OWN deadline surfaces `ScopeTimeout`,
  while a deadline on an ENCLOSING scope still reaches the wait and propagates
  through the inner scope as `Cancelled`. Spawn ownership (which coroutines a
  deadline reports and severs) is unchanged.
