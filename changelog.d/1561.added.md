- **comptime: construct enum variants and generic-struct instances by reflection**
  (I#1561, epic #1248, decisions/lang/1561-reflect-construct-enum-generic.md). Two
  additions to the reflection construct surface a decoder needs: (1) a new
  `reflect.construct_variant(T, comptime index, payload…)` builds the index-th
  variant of enum `T` (the enum counterpart of `reflect.construct`), lowering to
  `ConcreteEnum.Variant(payload…)`; and (2) `reflect.construct(T)` now also
  zero-constructs a **generic struct instance** (`Pair[int64, str]`), substituting
  the instance's type arguments into each field, not only a non-generic struct.
  Together these let a library derive generator decode an open enum
  (`@derive(Json)` / #1556) and a generic struct — the write duals of
  `reflect.field` — which were previously `unknown name` / `not supported yet`
  errors. No new codegen; the built-in codecs and prelude are untouched.
