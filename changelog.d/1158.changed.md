- **`net.connect_deadline` / `net.connect_ip_deadline` are now keyword-callable**
  (I#1158, decisions/stdlib/1158-connect-deadline-kwargs.md) with the deadline
  argument named **`deadline_ns`** (an absolute `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`-nanosecond
  bound, matching `net.set_deadline_ns`): `net.connect_deadline(host=...,
  port=..., deadline_ns=...)` and the `connect_ip_deadline` equivalent now
  type-check. Both had been positional-only pending a settled name. Positional
  calls are unchanged. `net` remains Experimental.
