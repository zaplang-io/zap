- **A method call on a null user reference-struct receiver now raises a located
  `NullReferenceError` at the call site** (I#1541, Epic I#1489,
  decisions/lang/1489-null-reference-error.md). `n.method()` on a null
  non-`Optional` `struct` receiver previously faulted only transitively (at the
  first `self.<field>` access inside the method body, or not at all for a method
  that never touches `self`); it now raises eagerly at the call site, e.g. `null
  dereference calling method 'inc' on 'Node' at foo.zap:14`. `value struct`
  receivers (inline), `Optional[T]` (its own `None`/unwrap path), and enum
  receivers (whose unit variants are a legal null) are unaffected.
