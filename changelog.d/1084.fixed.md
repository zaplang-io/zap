- **Native bigint division no longer scans the dividend bit-by-bit** (I#1084,
  decisions/runtime/1084-bigint-division-algorithm.md). `//` and `%` on bigints
  now use a word-wise long division — a single-limb scalar fast path and Knuth
  Algorithm D for multi-limb divisors — so a near-equal-length division with a
  small quotient costs ~O(limbs) instead of O(numerator-bits). The old kernel
  walked every numerator bit and shifted every remainder limb on each step, which
  made the canonical 10,000-digit CLBG pidigits benchmark exceed 30 minutes
  (zap-benchmarks#12). Floor/modulo semantics, divisor-signed remainders, the
  catchable `ZeroDivisionError` on divide-by-zero, and the limb representation are
  all unchanged.
