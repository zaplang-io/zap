- **Scheduler-deadline end-to-end fixtures no longer starve in the widened CI
  pool** (I#1317, regression from I#1305). After the pool widened to `$(nproc)`,
  wall-clock-deadline / cancellation programs (`scope_timeout*`, `spawn_forms_timeout`
  conformance fixtures and the net/TLS `*_deadline`/`*_timeout`/`*_cancel` goldens)
  ran in the wide lane and missed their scheduler-enforced deadline under CPU
  starvation, tripping the 60s `[run]` ceiling and bouncing unrelated PRs from the
  merge queue. `is_sensitive_name` now also treats `timeout`/`deadline`/`cancel` as
  conservative-lane markers, and `scope_cleanup` (no marker word) carries a
  `.serial` sidecar. Test-harness only; no language, library or runtime change.
