- **`net` — UDP multicast** (I#1142, decisions/stdlib/1142-multicast.md,
  follow-up to #991/#994). Join/leave a multicast group and set the senders'
  TTL/loopback, family-dispatched on the socket's IPv4/IPv6 (`udp_socket` vs
  `udp_socket6`): `net.join_multicast(sock, group)` /
  `net.leave_multicast(sock, group)` (default interface),
  `net.set_multicast_ttl(sock, hops)` (0..255) and
  `net.set_multicast_loopback(sock, on)`. The group literal must parse in the
  socket's family; a malformed group or an out-of-range TTL raises `ValueError`,
  and a `setsockopt` failure (e.g. no multicast route) raises a catchable error.
  Uses the structured `ip_mreq`/`ipv6_mreq` options with the platform-correct
  scalar width for the IPv4 TTL/loopback. An explicit-interface selector is a
  follow-up. `net` remains Experimental.
