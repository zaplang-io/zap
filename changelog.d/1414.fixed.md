- **A parked `select` is now woken by a scope cancellation or `scope(timeout=)`
  deadline** (I#1414, decisions/runtime/1414-select-scope-cancel.md). A `select`
  with no `default` and no ready case, parked inside a `scope:`, now wakes when
  that scope (or any enclosing one) is cancelled or its deadline fires — running
  its `finally` / `with` cleanup and raising, exactly as a bare channel
  send/receive in the same position already did. Previously the parked `select`
  registered no scope-cancellation waiter at all, so a cancel never reached it: it
  woke only when a real case fired, or after the grace-window sever (#825), and
  its cleanup never ran on cancellation. Like the other cancellation points
  (I#1398), a `select` binds to the **innermost** lexical `scope:` it is executing
  in, so D-0094 labelling holds in both directions — a scope's OWN deadline
  surfaces `ScopeTimeout`, an ENCLOSING cancel surfaces `Cancelled`. No surface
  change; a ready case or a `default` is unaffected.
