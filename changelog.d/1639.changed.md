- **`@derive` instantiation backtrace links a failing nested type to the outer field that required it** (I#1639, epic #1248, decisions/lang/1639-cross-type-derive-backtrace.md). When deriving a nested type `Inner` fails and `Inner` was pulled in by a field of an enclosing type `Outer` that also derives the trait, the `required because of …` chain now prepends the outer field — `required because of field `inner` of `Mid`` / `field `mid` of `Outer`` — giving the full `Outer → … → Inner` path above `Inner`'s own within-type chain. It crosses container levels (`list`/`dict`/`Optional`/tuple) and names the variant for an enum-outer payload. A standalone derive failure is unchanged. Diagnostic surface, Experimental.
