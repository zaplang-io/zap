- **Compile-time evaluation engine — pure tree-walking evaluator (Tier B1, E2)**
  (I#1250, epic #1249, decisions/lang/1250-comptime-evaluator.md). The greenfield
  core that turns the E1 `reflect.type_info(T)` value (#1257) into executable
  compile-time code: a new host-side, no-LLVM crate (`zapc_comptime`) that
  interprets a restricted, deterministic subset of Zap over comptime values —
  `int64`/`bool`/`str`/`char`, `list`/`tuple`, and the E1 reflection records —
  with `let` bindings, `comptime if`, field-access-by-index, arithmetic/
  comparison/boolean/string ops, and non-recursive user-function calls. `int64`
  arithmetic is bit-for-bit runtime-faithful (two's-complement wrap, floor
  division/modulo, the `INT64_MIN` edges); every out-of-fragment construct (I/O,
  FFI, spawn/channels, mutation, dict/set iteration, recursion, `match`, float/
  bigint) is a clean span-attributed `not available at comptime` error, never a
  panic. Compiler-internal and host-tested; there is **no `comptime` keyword
  surface yet** (wired by #1251) and no behavior change for existing programs.
