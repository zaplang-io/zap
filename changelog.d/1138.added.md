- **`net.connect_deadline(host, port, deadline_ns)` — hostname-aware bounded TCP
  dial** (I#1138, decisions/stdlib/1138-connect-deadline-hostname.md). A TCP
  connect to a **DNS hostname** bounded by one absolute `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`-ns
  deadline (from `time.monotonic_ns()`) that covers the whole operation —
  resolution and every resolved-address attempt. Fills the gap between
  `net.connect(host, port)` (hostname, no deadline) and
  `net.connect_ip_deadline(ip, port, deadline_ns)` (numeric IP only): neither
  could bound a lookup-plus-dial under a single budget. Expiry raises a catchable
  `TimeoutError`, distinct from a DNS failure, a connect failure, and
  cancellation (`Cancelled`); a zero deadline is unbounded, a negative one is a
  `ValueError`. `net` remains Experimental.
