- **`round(x[, ndigits])` and `pow(base, exp[, mod])` built-ins** (PR#1381, decisions/lang/1381-round-pow-builtins.md). `round` uses Python's banker's rounding (ties-to-even) — distinct from the float-domain `math.round` (half-away-from-zero): `round(float)` → `int64`, `round(float, ndigits)` → `float64`, `round(int[, ndigits])` → integer (power-of-ten rounding for negative `ndigits`). `pow(base, exp)` is exactly `base ** exp` over the numeric tower; `pow(base, exp, mod)` is integer modular exponentiation. Out-of-range `round`, and a zero modulus or negative exponent for 3-arg `pow`, raise catchable `ArithmeticError`/`ValueError`.
