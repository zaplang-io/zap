- **Complex array comparisons and reductions no longer silently corrupt or link-fail;
  they are rejected cleanly** (I#1721, sibling of I#1719). On an `array[complex]`,
  comparisons `== != < <= > >=` used to read the 16-byte complex element as a raw
  `int64` (`zap_array_cmp` has no complex case) → garbage; and `min`/`max`/`prod`/`mean`
  (and axis-`sum`) either emitted an undefined `zap_array_{…}_complex` (link error) or
  routed to the axis-reduction kernel, which has no complex case and left the result
  buffer unfilled. These now reject at compile time — ordering and `min`/`max` as
  "complex arrays have no ordering" (as `sort`/`argsort` already do), and `==`/`!=`,
  `prod`, `mean` and axis reductions as "not yet supported for complex arrays". Full
  `sum(a)` over a complex array is unchanged and still supported. Real kernels for the
  deferred cases are tracked under the complex-array work (I#938).
