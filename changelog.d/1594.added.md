- **serde breadth: `uint64`, `list[T]`, and `dict[str, V]` fields** (I#1594, epic
  #1552, decisions/lang/1594-serde-breadth-collections.md). The bundled `serde`
  foundation (#1570) now (de)serializes unsigned integers and scalar-element
  collections in addition to the earlier scalar + nested-struct surface:
  `serde.Serializer` gains `emit_uint` and `begin_seq`/`end_seq`/`begin_map`/
  `end_map`; `serde.Deserializer` gains `read_uint` and `begin_seq`/`begin_map`
  (returning the element/entry count) plus `end_seq`/`end_map`; and the generic
  `serialize`/`deserialize` drivers gain the `uint64`, `list[T]`, and
  `dict[str, V]` branches. Unblocked by the comptime-for dispatch fix (#1592).
  Downstream codecs (e.g. `zaplang-io/msgpack`) need a matching backend update.
