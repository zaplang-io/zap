- **`str.center` splits odd padding like CPython** (I#1656,
  decisions/stdlib/1656-center-odd-padding-split.md, superseding D-0210's center
  split). When the total width and the margin are both odd, the extra fill now
  goes on the **left** (`"hi".center(5)` → `"  hi "`, was `" hi  "`), matching
  CPython's `str.center`. Even-padding results, `ljust`/`rjust`, and the
  multibyte-fill behavior (#1648) are unchanged.
