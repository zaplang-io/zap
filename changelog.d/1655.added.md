- **The argparse-backed handler adapter now binds `char` and `bytes` handler
  parameters** (I#1655, decisions/lang/1655-namespace-nonnumeric-getters.md). The
  object-model `Namespace` gains two typed getters — `get_bytes(name)` (the
  argument string's UTF-8 encoding) and `get_char(name)` (a single Unicode
  codepoint; a non-single-character argument raises a catchable `ValueError`) —
  and a handler declaring `char`/`bytes` parameters adapts through them
  (`show$impl(ns.get_char("c"), ns.get_bytes("b"))`) instead of being a
  decoration-site `ZAP5048`. Unlike the narrowing numeric scalars (#1625) these
  read the typed getter directly, with no conversion wrapper. Part of epic #1644;
  `int8`/`int16` (#1654) and `bigint`/`complex` (blocked on the missing
  `str`-parse conversions, #1657) remain a precise `ZAP5048`.
