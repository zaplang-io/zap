- **`net.port` on a Unix-domain socket now raises `ValueError`** (I#1159,
  decisions/stdlib/1159-netport-unix.md) instead of returning a meaningless number
  read from the `sockaddr_un` path. A Unix socket has no port; `net.port` now
  raises a catchable `ValueError` for it, while AF_INET / AF_INET6 sockets still
  return their bound port. `net` remains Experimental.
