- **A blocking `select` no longer corrupts memory when woken under multiple
  worker Ms** (I#1435, decisions/runtime/1435-select-park-mutex-flush.md). A
  `select` that parks allocates its park mutex on the select'ing coroutine's
  stack frame; the channel counterpart that claims a case woke the coroutine
  (`coready`) while still holding that mutex and unlocked it only afterwards, so
  with `MAXPROCS > 1` the woken coroutine could resume on another M and reuse the
  frame before the claimer's trailing unlock — scribbling pthread-mutex bytes
  over the reused stack. It surfaced as a recurring aarch64 merge-queue flake:
  the `time.ticker` / `time.after` + `select` goldens aborted with glibc
  `tpp.c:86 (__pthread_tpp_change_priority)` (the reused mutex's `__kind` read as
  `PTHREAD_PRIO_PROTECT`), and on x86 as a corrupted allocation size in the next
  `spawn`. `zap_select` now re-acquires and releases the park mutex before
  destroying it, flushing the waking claimer; a new `time_ticker_select_stress`
  golden exercises thousands of claim/resume cycles under `MAXPROCS > 1`.
