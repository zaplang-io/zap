- **Enum `@derive` breadth + variant-payload diagnostics**
  (I#1375, epic #1371, decisions/lang/1375-enum-derive-breadth-diagnostics.md). A
  derive failure a generator raises inside an enum variant payload now renders
  the consumer `error[ZAP7003]: cannot derive …` pointing at that payload field,
  with a `required because of field `<name>` of `<Enum>`` frame — the enum analog
  of the struct-field attribution (previously the chain had no frame for an enum
  because the variant unroll passed no fields). The covered enum-deriving surface
  is locked by goldens: tier-1 constant and payload enums, a trait derived across
  struct and enum, nested-struct recursion, and deterministic declaration-order
  iteration via `comptime v.index`. Two breadth cases that need core fixes are
  filed, not worked around: a method call on an enum-typed variant-pattern capture
  (I#1401) and generic-enum variant construction (I#1402).
