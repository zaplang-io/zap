- **`str(bytes, encoding[, errors])` decode** (PR#1459, decisions/lang/1459-str-bytes-decode.md). Explicit decode of a `bytes` value to a `str`: encodings `utf-8` / `ascii` / `latin-1` (with the usual aliases), and `errors` of `strict` (default), `replace` (U+FFFD), or `ignore`. An unknown encoding/errors handler, or a strict decode failure, raises a catchable `ValueError`. The 1-argument `str(bytes)` is unchanged (UTF-8/strict). Batch B of the overload-parity epic #1386.
