- **`str.expandtabs` counts columns in codepoints** (I#1635, epic #1638). The
  column counter advanced once per **byte**, so a tab stop landed in the wrong
  place after a multibyte character (`"é\tA".expandtabs(8)` emitted 6 spaces;
  now 7, matching CPython). `\n`/`\r` still reset the column and `tabsize <= 0`
  still drops tabs. ASCII results are unchanged.
