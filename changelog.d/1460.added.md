- **`list(iterable)` and `dict(pairs / copy / **kwargs)` constructors** (PR#1460, decisions/lang/1460-list-dict-constructors.md). `list(iterable)` materializes any iterable into an ordered `list[T]` (the twin of `set(iterable)`). `dict(...)` builds a dict from an iterable of `(key, value)` 2-tuples, copies another dict, or collects keyword arguments into a `str`-keyed dict (`dict(x=1, y=2)`); positional and keyword forms cannot be mixed. The subscripted `list[T]()` / `dict[K,V]()` empty-collection constructors are unchanged. Completes the mechanical batch of the overload-parity epic #1386 (`tuple(iterable)` remains deferred, #1461).
