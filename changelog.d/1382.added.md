- **`Iterator[T]` / `Iterable[T]` interfaces (foundation)** (PR#1382, decisions/lang/1382-iterator-protocol.md, RFC discussion #1417). The nameable, generic structural interfaces for lazy iteration — `Iterable[T]` requires `__iter__`, `Iterator[T]` requires the comma-ok `__next__(self) -> (T, bool)` — are now predefined (the first *generic* compiler-known interfaces) and conform structurally at the element type. The `iter()`/`next()` built-ins, precise reified per-container iterators, and value-position element-type enforcement are follow-up phases of #1382.
