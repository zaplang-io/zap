- **The argparse-backed handler adapter now binds wider scalar parameter types**
  (I#1625, decisions/lang/1625-adapter-wide-scalars.md). Beyond
  `int64`/`float64`/`bool`/`str`, a narrower scalar reads the wide `Namespace`
  getter and converts: `int32`/`uint8`/`uint16`/`uint32`/`uint64`/`usize` →
  `intN(ns.get_int("p"))` and `float32` → `float32(ns.get_float("p"))`. So a
  handler such as `def cmd(int32 n, uint8 level)` now adapts where it was a
  decoration-site `ZAP5048` before. `int8`/`int16` (no scalar conversion builtin)
  and `char`/`bytes`/`bigint`/`complex` remain unsupported.
