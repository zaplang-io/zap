- **`str.translate` keys by whole codepoint** (I#1637,
  decisions/stdlib/1637-translate-codepoint-keys.md, superseding D-0214's
  byte-keyed implementation clause). Multibyte character keys in the
  `dict[str, str]` never matched, so non-ASCII characters could not be remapped
  or deleted (`"café".translate({"é": "e"})` was a no-op; now `"cafe"`). Lookups
  are now per codepoint, matching CPython's per-character translation; the
  `dict[str, str]` surface and ASCII behavior are unchanged.
