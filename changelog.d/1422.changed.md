- **Coroutine timers free their worker on Windows too** (I#1422,
  decisions/runtime/1422-windows-iocp-timer.md). A coroutine `time.sleep` /
  `time.after` / `time.ticker` now parks on the fd-less scheduler timer under the
  Windows IOCP poller, freeing its worker M for the wait, instead of taking the
  thread-pinning blocking fallback. The IOCP poll loop already drives the shared
  deadline min-heap (I#1410) and wakes via `PostQueuedCompletionStatus`, so the
  fd-less timer park is simply un-gated for IOCP — no surface or behavior change
  beyond Windows no longer blocking a worker per coroutine timer. Platforms with
  no deadline-scanning poller keep the blocking fallback.
