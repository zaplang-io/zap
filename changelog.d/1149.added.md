- **`datetime` civil-time resolution across DST gaps and folds** (I#1149,
  decisions/stdlib/1149-named-zone-retention.md). New surface, built on the
  bundled IANA data (#1045):
  - `datetime.resolve(tz, year, month, day, hour, minute, second, fold=0)` —
    *localize* a wall clock into a zone, classifying a gap or an overlap and
    selecting the instant with `fold` (0 = earlier, 1 = later on an overlap;
    0 = pre, 1 = post offset in a gap, PEP 495). The TimeZone is positional, the
    civil fields are positional ints, and `fold` is an optional trailing int.
  - `datetime.is_ambiguous(dt)` / `datetime.is_imaginary(dt)` — whether `dt`'s
    wall clock occurs twice (fall-back) or never (spring-forward) in its retained
    zone (dateutil `datetime_ambiguous` / the negation of `datetime_exists`).
  - `datetime.resolve_imaginary(dt)` — shift an imaginary (gap) wall clock forward
    by the gap length onto the real post-transition time; a no-op for a real time
    (dateutil `resolve_imaginary`).

  These match CPython `zoneinfo` / `python-dateutil` semantics and are
  deterministic against the toolchain's bundled tables, independent of the host.
