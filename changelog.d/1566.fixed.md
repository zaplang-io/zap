- **Recoverable input/state errors are catchable** (#1566,
  decisions/lang/1566-catchable-input-errors.md). Ordinary input or state faults
  that previously aborted or `exit(1)`-ed the process — even inside a matching
  `try`/`except` — now raise a catchable exception that the local handler
  catches, with `finally` and `with` cleanup running on the unwind path.
  First cut: `str.index`/`rindex` and `chr`/`char` (→ `ValueError`); `bytes[i]`,
  `deque` pop/popleft/get/`d[i]`, and `heap` pop/peek (→ `IndexError`); `str`,
  `bytes`, `list`, and `tuple` slices with a zero step (→ `ValueError`);
  `random.randint` reversed bounds and `random.choice` of an empty sequence;
  invalid regex patterns / replacement groups (→ `ValueError`, catchable where
  they previously `exit(1)`-ed); and the missing unwind edges on `AtomicArray`
  negative capacity, `sync.add`/`done`/`rlock_unlock` misuse, `argparse`
  duplicate registration, `yaml.load` / derived `from_yaml`/`from_json`/`from_csv`
  malformed input, `print` of a Python object whose `__str__` raises, and
  function-key (composite / float) dict & set operations. The uncaught form still
  reports the normal diagnostic and exits 1. Numeric `array[T]` N-D indexing and
  slicing bounds are a sequenced follow-up; empty `heap.replace`, CSPRNG
  entropy-failure, and the `MemoryError`/fail-stop reconciliation are flagged for
  a separate policy decision.
