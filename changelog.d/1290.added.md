- **User-extensible `@derive` — `@derivable(Trait)` registration model (E3a)**
  (I#1290, epic #1289 / #1248, decisions/lang/1290-derivable-registration.md).
  The derivable-trait set is no longer a closed list: `@derive(X)` now resolves
  against a registry seeded with the compiler built-ins (`Eq`/`Ord`/`Json`/
  `Csv`/`Yaml`/`Repr`/`Hash`, D-0036, pre-registered for back-compat) and
  extended by a library `@derivable(Trait)` on a generic generator function,
  order-independently across the module. A library trait resolves cleanly in a
  `@derive`, is surfaced on `Reflector::library_derivables()` for the generator
  (E3b, #1291) to consume, and is reported by lowering as *deferred to E3b*
  rather than "unknown derivable trait". Diagnostics: `ZAP5057` ("invalid
  @derivable") for a malformed registration, a non-generic generator, a
  collision with a built-in, or a duplicate trait; `@derive` of an unregistered
  trait stays `ZAP5035`. Registration + resolution only — no forwarding-impl
  synthesis or codegen yet (E3b). No behavior change for existing programs.
