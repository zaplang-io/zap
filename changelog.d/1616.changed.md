- **`@derive` instantiation backtrace names the enum variant** (I#1616, epic #1553, decisions/lang/1616-enum-derive-backtrace-variant.md). When a `@derive` failure's `required because of …` chain anchors on an enum variant payload field, the frame now reads `payload field `r` of variant `Circle` of `Shape`` instead of mislabeling it `field `r` of `Shape`` — so the chain says which variant carried the failing type. Struct-field frames are unchanged. Diagnostic surface, Experimental.
