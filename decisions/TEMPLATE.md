# Title

**Status:** Proposed

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences. Be concrete — a reader must be able to
tell what is now true without reading the rationale.

## Rationale

Why this and not the alternatives. List the options rejected and the reason each
lost. This is the part that stops the question being re-litigated.

## Consequences

What this commits us to, what it rules out, and any follow-up it implies.
